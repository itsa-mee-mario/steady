# Steady: what changed

## Easier to use
- **Speak button first.** One big "Speak" button. Typing is still there, just smaller.
- **Hindi.** English / हिन्दी toggle at the top. Voice listens in Indian English or Hindi.
- **Simpler Home.** Home shows only your buttons and the "right now" area. Everything else moved to a new **Settings** tab.
- **Text size.** A− / A+ buttons at the top, four sizes, and everything scales (header, menu, chips).
- **Bigger buttons.** Every button is at least 56px tall.
- **High contrast.** A ◐ button at the top. The "right now" box has a thicker border.
- **Plain words.** "Record" is now "What changed". "Install fix" is now "Update my phone". "Rule" wording is gone.
- **Your own names.** Change "Mom" and "Alex" in Settings.

## Safer
- **Medicine reminder asks Morning or Evening** (no more guessing AM/PM). It repeats daily and has a "Mark as taken" button.
- **"Forget everything" asks first.**

## Easier to hear and read
- **🔊 Listen** buttons read replies and family notes aloud.
- **Messages stay 10 seconds** (before: under 3) and can be tapped away.
- Replies on Home are now visible after you approve something.
- The screen keeps its scroll spot and focus when it updates.
- "Keep it" is now two big buttons: **Keep always** / **Keep on one day**.

## Family phone (new)
1. Parent: Settings, then "Make a family code".
2. Child: open the same page, switch to Helper, type the code.
3. Child sends a button name and optional note.
4. Parent taps **Yes** and it becomes a button. Pressing it shows the note in big text.
- Optional: "Add family buttons without asking me" (off by default).
- Needs the published Claude link and a signed-in child. It does not work from the files on your computer.

## Code
- Split into `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`.
- `replay-tour.js` now reads `app.js`. All 18 tour steps still pass.
